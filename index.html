Where to buy your ticket at Incheon Airport

Terminal 1: Arrivals level (1F), ticket counters inside near Exits 4 and 9, or outdoor ticket counters near Exits 4, 6, 7, 8, 11 and 13. Buy a ticket at a counter or self-service machine before boarding.

Terminal 2: Go to the Transportation Center on B1. Buy your ticket at the bus ticket counter or ticket kiosk, then proceed to the bus platform.
